Qualitative analysis in chemistry involves the systematic identification of cations and anions present in an inorganic salt sample. Traditionally, this is achieved by performing preliminary tests (such as color, flame test, and solubility) followed by confirmatory tests using group reagents. <br><br>
In a Virtual Reality (VR) laboratory, students perform the same steps in a simulated environment. They can pick up test tubes, reagents, burners, and observe reactions like precipitation, color change, or gas evolution virtually.<br><br>
<b>This method provides:</b><br>
- A safe and eco-friendly environment (no harmful chemicals).<br>
- Repeatability without wastage of reagents.<br>
- Cost-effective, interactive, and engaging learning.<br><br>
Thus, VR-based qualitative analysis allows students to practice systematically identifying unknown salts without physical hazards while still learning real chemical concepts.
