### About the Experiment 

The actual lab pain point in chemistry qualitative analysis is centered on risk and resource management. Procedures like the Flame Test necessitate the use of hazardous materials,
specifically concentrated hydrochloric acid for cleaning the wire loop, posing a burn risk and requiring strict fume hood ventilation. Furthermore, inexperienced users risk damaging
expensive platinum wire loops or incorrectly disposing of metallic salt residues. The learning process is often inhibited by the immediate, fleeting nature of the flame color and the fact that the crucial sub-atomic process (electron excitement and de-excitement) is completely invisible.<br>
The VR innovation completely eliminates these concerns, shifting the focus entirely to conceptual mastery. The virtual environment provides risk-free interaction, allowing students
to experiment with toxic or explosive mixtures and flammable reagents without any physical danger. Critically, the VR system can directly visualize the invisible, such as the real-time
movement of electrons causing the color change, linking the observable macro-phenomenon to the micro-level theory. This enhanced visualization and infinite, safe repetition accelerates
student understanding and mastery of qualitative identification
